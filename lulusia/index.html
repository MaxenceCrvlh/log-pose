Lulusia Existed is a small One Piece TCG shop specialising in Japanese cards.

Every Monday, Wednesday and Friday we post a Guess the Card short video: a card is hidden behind scrolling poneglyph symbols and you guess which one it is in the comments.

Lulusia Publisher is our own small tool that publishes these videos to our own Instagram and YouTube accounts and posts the answer in the comments. It is not offered to other users.
