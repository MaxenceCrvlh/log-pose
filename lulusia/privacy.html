Privacy policy

Lulusia Publisher — last updated 2 November 2026

What this app is

Lulusia Publisher is a private tool used only by Lulusia Existed to publish our own videos to our own Instagram, Facebook Page, YouTube channel and TikTok account (TikTok: videos are sent as drafts that we publish ourselves), and to post replies and answers in the comments of those videos. It is not available to the public and no other person signs in to it.

Data we access

  • Our own account information (account name, channel name, follower count) to check the connection.
  • Comments posted publicly on our own videos (text and public username), only to detect correct guesses, reply to them, and keep a simple count of correct guesses per username for a leaderboard.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not access private messages, contacts, or accounts other than our own.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access keys and the small list of posted videos and correct-guess counts are stored only on our own computer. Comment data older than a few weeks is no longer used. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page or Meta's business integrations settings.

Google API Services

Lulusia Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
